About PassItOn

Learn how the platform works and why PassItOn is the smarter way to share and claim items.

Our mission

PassItOn is a community-driven platform designed to help people share, swap, and find useful items without the hassle of traditional buying and selling. We make it easy to list items you no longer need and connect them with people who can benefit from them.

How it works

List items with a photo, description, and price. Other users can reserve available items, and once an item is claimed, the owner can mark it as received to complete the exchange. Everything is handled through a simple, clean web interface.

Why PassItOn?

We believe in creating a friendly environment for sharing and reusing items. PassItOn reduces waste, saves money, and helps people get useful products from within their community.
